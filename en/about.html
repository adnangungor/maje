Heritage & Principles

Statutory mastery, integrity, and institutional diligence since 1992.

Maje strengthens the legal and operational foundations of capital investments in Turkey through over three decades of consulting heritage and dual-hub operations in Ankara and Istanbul.

1992

Founded in Ankara: Maje Advisory was founded in the capital, establishing core capabilities in government relations and investment incentive legislation.

2001

Istanbul Branch Opened: Expanded operations to Turkey's commercial and industrial epicenter to serve multinational clients and regional headquarters.

2004

Customs Brokerage Established: Established dedicated customs brokerage operations to seamlessly integrate advisory with physical port clearance.
