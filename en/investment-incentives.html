Investment Incentives Advisory

Managing the investment incentive lifecycle from initiation to closure.

For international and domestic corporations investing in greenfield plants, modernization, or expansion projects in Turkey; we provide rigorous statutory consulting, government relations, and E-TUYS digital administration.

01 — 8-Step Methodology

Comprehensive Investment Incentives Lifecycle

1

Regulatory Screening

Initial Assessment

In-depth statutory review of Turkish investment legislation to evaluate sectoral applicability, regional tiered benefits (Regions 1 to 6), and maximum support thresholds.

2

Project Structuring

Technical Modeling

Structuring procurement lists, machinery classifications, and financial expenditures in exact accordance with statutory incentive eligibility rules.

3

System Identification

Regime Optimization

Determining the most suitable incentive framework based on investment volume, technological depth, and value-add: modeling statutory contribution across the Türkiye Century Development Initiative (Technology, Strategic, and Local Development Tracks), Sectoral & Target Investments, Priority Investments, or Regional Support Instruments.

4

Presentation & Defense

Administrative Briefing

Preparing economic justification briefs, technological novelty reports, and employment projections for submission to ministerial review boards.

5

Application Dossier

E-TUYS Platform

Formulating statutory documentation, domestic and imported machinery registries, and environmental commitments within the Ministry of Industry and Technology's digital system.

6

Filing & Certification

Ministry Relations

Executing formal application procedures in Ankara and managing bureaucratic inquiries through to official Investment Incentive Certificate issuance.

7

Amendment & Endorsement

Lifecycle Operations

Managing ongoing operational updates: machinery additions/swaps, project duration extensions, capacity adjustments, and financial leasing endorsements.

8

Completion Visa

Audit & Closure

Coordinating field expert audits upon project completion, verifying tax deductions, releasing bank guarantees, and closing out all statutory liabilities.

Corporate Enablement

Statutory Compliance Training

We provide tailored coaching sessions for your local accounting, finance, and procurement departments. Our team ensures all VAT exemptions, corporate tax reductions, customs duty waivers, and social security premium subsidies are booked in rigorous compliance with Turkish tax auditor expectations.
