Personal Data Protection and Privacy Notice

Information regarding the processing of your personal data under the Turkish Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

1. Data Controllers

Under the Law on Protection of Personal Data No. 6698, your personal data may be processed by Maje Advisory Industry & Trade Co. Ltd. and Maje Customs Brokerage Co. Ltd. in the capacity of joint data controllers as detailed below.

2. Purposes of Processing

Corporate identity and contact information submitted via our consultation request forms, email, or telephone channels (name, email, phone number, company title, and inquiry details) are processed strictly to evaluate your advisory requirements, manage business proposals, and fulfill statutory compliance obligations.

3. Legal Grounds

Your personal data is gathered electronically through form submissions based on the statutory grounds stipulated under Article 5 of Law No. 6698: “Processing of data is necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller, provided that it does not violate the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject” and “Direct relevance to the establishment or performance of a contract.”

4. Data Subject Rights

Pursuant to Article 11 of the Law, data subjects are entitled to inquire whether their personal data is processed, request information regarding processing, verify compliance with intended purposes, and request rectification of inaccurate data.
